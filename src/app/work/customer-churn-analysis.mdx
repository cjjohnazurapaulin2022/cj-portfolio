## The Problem
A subscription-based company was losing 15% of customers monthly without understanding why. They needed a predictive model to identify at-risk customers before they churned.

## Solution Overview
Built a machine learning classification model that identifies customers with high churn probability, enabling proactive retention efforts.

## Process

### Data Collection & Exploration
- Gathered 6 months of customer data: usage patterns, support tickets, payment history
- 50,000+ customers with 25+ features
- Exploratory analysis revealed key churn indicators

### Feature Engineering
Created features including:
- Days since last login
- Number of support tickets
- Average session duration
- Payment delay frequency
- Feature usage adoption rate

### Model Development
Tested multiple algorithms:
- Logistic Regression (baseline: 78% accuracy)
- Random Forest (85% accuracy)
- XGBoost (84% accuracy)

### Results
- **85% accuracy** in predicting churn within 30 days
- **Identified top 3 churn drivers:** low login frequency, unresolved support tickets, payment delays
- **Recommended actions** reduced churn by 25% in pilot program
